# Kitsch blog + AEO strategy

Accessed September 8, 2026. Public-observation audit; no Search Console, analytics, backlink or revenue data was available.

## What is working

- Commercial architecture maps clearly to customer problems and categories: hair loss, damage, color safety, curls, fragrance, bars, styling and overnight protection.
- The visible May 2026 cluster builds topical depth around shampoo-bar education, syndet science, scalp concerns, hair growth and travel.
- September’s visible cluster expands into practical high-intent answers: heatless curls, towel choice, cost per wash, satin pillowcases, claw-clip styles and troubleshooting.
- Current editorial inclusion for hair perfume and heatless curlers creates credible third-party discovery signals.

## Risks to validate

- Publishing appears bursty: eight visible posts on May 18 and eight on September 4. A steady refresh-and-publish rhythm would make ownership and learning clearer.
- Several shampoo-bar articles may overlap on intent. Validate cannibalization in Search Console before consolidating.
- Evidence-heavy claims should consistently show cited sources, reviewer credentials, last-reviewed dates and carefully scoped product language.
- The homepage exposed duplicate Organization and WebSite schema blocks in the observed markup. Confirm whether both are intentional.

## 90-day operating plan

1. Export query/page data from Search Console; cluster by intent and identify cannibalization.
2. Select five canonical hubs: hair perfume, heatless curls, shampoo bars, satin vs silk, overnight protection.
3. Add quotable answer blocks, comparison tables, audience variants, FAQs, expert review and consistent structured data.
4. Reuse each answer in PDP FAQs, creator briefs, lifecycle email and retail education.
5. Review monthly: non-brand impressions, top-three visibility, answer-engine citations, assisted conversion, freshness and content decay.
