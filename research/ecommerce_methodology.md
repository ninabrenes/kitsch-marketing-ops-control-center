# Public e-commerce methodology

Accessed September 8, 2026. This is a public merchandising and demand-signal snapshot, not an audited sales report.

## Gross retail sales-value proxy

`displayed units × observed price` is used only where TikTok Shop shows a cumulative sold counter or Target shows a “bought in last month” threshold. For Target, the badge floor is used, so the calculation is a minimum at the displayed price. For TikTok Shop, the calculation combines a lifetime-to-date rounded unit counter with the price visible on the access date.

This proxy is not Kitsch revenue, GMV reported by Kitsch, net sales, wholesale revenue, contribution margin, or a period-matched measure. It does not account for historical price changes, coupons, bundles, refunds, returns, taxes, shipping, marketplace fees, creator commissions, retailer margin, wholesale terms, or units sold through other channels. Values are directional and should not be summed across channels as company revenue.

## Product universe

The product matrix covers representative hero and high-signal products visible across Kitsch DTC best sellers, TikTok Shop, Target, Ulta, and Walmart. It is not a complete catalog: current public pages displayed approximately 55 DTC best sellers, 203 Ulta listings, 144 Target listings, and 90 Walmart listings.
