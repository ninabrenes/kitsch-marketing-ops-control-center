# Brand strategy + open-source enablement

Independent research pass completed September 8, 2026. Facts are grounded in Kitsch-owned pages and verified GitHub repositories; implications are hypotheses.

## Brand findings

1. Kitsch owns accessible elevation: practical, desirable beauty essentials that improve ordinary routines without added effort.
2. The portfolio is a routine ecosystem across Hair Care, Style, Skin, Shower and Sleep. Measure need state × franchise × channel × evergreen/drop.
3. Differentiation combines functional problem-solving, cultural relevance and low-friction sustainability.
4. Consumables, fragrance, bundles, quiz, rewards, referrals and auto-ship indicate a shift from viral products toward a replenishable lifestyle system.
5. Distinctive warm editorial visual codes and direct benefit-first voice are strong; claim governance needs tightening where footprint or sustainability totals differ.

## Repository decisions

- Use [GoogleChrome/lighthouse](https://github.com/GoogleChrome/lighthouse) now for repeatable technical SEO, accessibility, performance and best-practice baselines.
- Use [agencyenterprise/aiseo-audit](https://github.com/agencyenterprise/aiseo-audit) as a secondary heuristic diagnostic only; its score is not citation probability.
- Use [MaartenGr/BERTopic](https://github.com/MaartenGr/BERTopic) only if the exported caption/blog corpus is large enough; otherwise retain the controlled manual taxonomy.
- Use [plotly/plotly.py](https://github.com/plotly/plotly.py) for portable interactive and static trend visuals when CSV exports are available.
- Treat [tiktok/ttspc-server-sample](https://github.com/tiktok/ttspc-server-sample) as future authenticated architecture reference only.

Avoid unofficial scraping dependencies in the unsolicited prototype. They add platform, privacy and maintenance risk without producing decision-grade first-party data.
