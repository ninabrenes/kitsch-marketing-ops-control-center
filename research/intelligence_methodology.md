# Intelligence methodology

Accessed September 8, 2026. This layer is a public-data prototype, not a substitute for Kitsch's analytics stack.

## Search demand

Google Trends was queried for United States Web Search, August 29, 2021 through August 30, 2026, for `hair perfume`, `heatless curls`, `shampoo bar`, `satin pillowcase`, and `hair oil`. The site visual uses monthly averages of Google's weekly 0–100 relative-interest index from January 2024 through August 2026. Values are normalized across the selected terms and window, not absolute search volume. Low-volume terms are compressed because `hair oil` is the largest series. Rising-query labels come from Google's related-query module.

The Social historical comparison uses equal elapsed windows: January–August 2025 versus January–August 2026. Its headline percentages compare the eight monthly index averages for each term. They describe change in the selected Trends index—not absolute search-volume growth, social performance, campaign lift, market share, or revenue. Satin pillowcase was excluded from the headline comparison because its low and frequently zero rounded index creates unstable percentage changes.

## Paid competitive signals

Google counts are approximate US-domain archive inventory observed in the official Ads Transparency Center with any-time, all-platform, all-format filters. Counts are not active-only, spend, performance, keyword coverage, or ROAS.

Meta counts and creative mix are third-party snapshots indexed from Motion or Brandsearch. They are directional pressure and messaging signals—not authenticated advertiser exports and not performance benchmarks. US commercial ad spend, impressions, targeting, Marketplace-placement delivery, clicks, and conversions are not public.

## Insight contract

Every smart insight is structured as: observed fact → interpretation → proposed decision → internal metric needed. Hypotheses remain labeled until first-party data validates them.
