# Kitsch Marketing Operations Control Center — Final Design QA

QA date: September 9, 2026

Prototype: `http://localhost:3000/`

Target: desktop 1440×1000 and mobile 390×844

## Evidence boundary

- Public profile counters, official site pages, captions, product pages and storefront counters are time-stamped public observations.
- Demo targets, example OKRs, benchmark scorecards and operating scenarios are visibly labeled illustrative or proposed.
- CAC, ROAS, MER, LTV, contribution, conversion, margin and customer-quality results remain blank or illustrative until Shopify, finance, Klaviyo, media, GA4/Search Console and Asana data are reconciled.
- Browser visual checks do not establish full WCAG or screen-reader compliance.

## Page-by-page audit

1. **Executive overview — Healthy.** Four business outcomes, public-vs-demo labels, decision briefs, channel logos, product pulse, funnel, OKR/Asana connection and data health are present. Desktop and mobile show no horizontal overflow or broken images.
2. **Growth signals — Healthy.** Hypotheses are qualitative, expandable and tied to validation logic instead of invented precision. Methodology is no longer the first substantive module.
3. **Commerce control — Healthy.** Product imagery, official links, Shopify/TikTok Shop context, metric definitions and demo OKRs are intact. Public proof is separated from internal economics.
4. **Creator operations — Healthy.** Creator evidence, roles, source links, economics contract and proposed operating measures are visible; unverified creator performance is not presented as fact.
5. **Customer journey — Healthy.** The Discover → Consider → Buy → Repeat journey is interactive and maps customer question, owner, metric and next action. Illustrative targets are labeled.
6. **Storefront + stack — Healthy.** Storefront stages, martech ownership and measurement handoffs are interactive and responsive.
7. **KPI definitions — Healthy.** Metric contracts expose formula, owner, source, freshness, approval and decision threshold. Actuals remain blank until connected.
8. **Launch control — Healthy.** Launch exceptions, recovery briefs, owners, due dates and evidence states replace generic status reporting.
9. **Social system — Healthy.** The page now leads with a ranked content-test queue. Channel logos are interactive; metrics are sorted business → journey → creative → output; benchmark logic and dummy values are explicitly labeled; eight content options span the funnel.
10. **Search + answers — Healthy.** The first substantive module shows eight explicit query patterns, search intent, current Kitsch result, public evidence link, opportunity and the missing rank/volume connection. A four-item content queue includes proposed owner, gate and measurement.
11. **Messaging playbook — Healthy.** The earlier stitched-page glitch was removed. The page is now a creative approval desk: public message evidence → pattern → channel adaptation → approve/revise/block decision. Supporting evidence is collapsed by default.
12. **Competitor map — Healthy.** Defend/differentiate/test lanes, evidence disclosures and plain-language actions are visible without using follower count as the strategy.
13. **Marketing operations — Healthy.** Exceptions and operating decisions appear before the role appendix. The identity workflow is a symmetric five-stage system—capture demand, anchor order, resolve consent, govern joins, read economics—with implementation IDs hidden on demand and a single-column mobile sequence.
14. **Sources + methods — Healthy.** Source lineage, confidence, limitation and refresh expectations remain accessible. Methodology is treated as evidence support, not the leadership story.

## Interaction and responsive checks

- All 14 navigation tabs successfully changed the visible page.
- Mobile navigation drawer opened and closed correctly.
- Desktop and mobile page sweeps returned zero horizontal overflow and zero broken images.
- Search keyword links, social platform controls, content-test disclosures, message decision briefs and identity ID disclosures use real links, buttons or semantic `details`/`summary` controls.
- The remaining visible logo link was increased to a 44px mobile target.
- Reduced-motion rules remain in place for animated disclosure arrows and hover motion.
