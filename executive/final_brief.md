# Kitsch Marketing Operations Control Center

## What I observed

1. Kitsch has a broad, accessible portfolio spanning hair care, fragrance, heatless styling, sleep, shower, skin and accessories.
2. TikTok Shop publicly displays 2.3M sold and a visible assortment led by repeatable routines and problem-solving products.
3. Hair perfume is a current cross-channel narrative across DTC newness, TikTok Shop and 2026 editorial coverage.
4. Bottle-free hair care leads the DTC best-seller ordering observed on September 8, 2026.
5. The brand has meaningful public communities across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest.
6. Retailer pages expose strong proof and different hero-product signals at Ulta and Target.
7. Kitsch appears in current editorial answers for hair perfume and heatless curling; shampoo-bar and satin-pillowcase visibility is more competitive or fragmented.
8. Public scale claims need one current master source: the supplied job description says 32,000+ retailers in 92 countries while an older site page says 20,000 in 27.

## What I would want to understand internally

How channel revenue and margin reconcile; which customer definition leadership trusts; repeat behavior by acquisition product; launch forecast accuracy; inventory constraints; creative taxonomy and attribution; retailer sell-through timing; and which operating friction consumes leadership time.

## What I would monitor weekly

Revenue and contribution by channel; blended CAC and MER; new vs returning mix; budget pacing; launch readiness and inventory risk; creative learning; CRM health; public demand shifts; and the decision queue.

## What I would operationalize

A KPI dictionary, single launch calendar, twice-weekly launch room during active windows, weekly executive pulse, decision log, monthly business review and quarterly priority-setting rhythm.

## What I would not assume

Public followers, reviews, retailer badges and TikTok Shop counters do not reveal Kitsch revenue, profitability, attribution, incrementality, customer quality, inventory or internal performance.

## First thirty days

Listen and map decisions; define sources and owners; pilot one launch and one weekly scorecard; close the loop with a retro; automate only trusted definitions.
