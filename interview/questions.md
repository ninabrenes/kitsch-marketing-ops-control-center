# Interview Questions

1. Which metrics does leadership trust most today—and where do definitions still vary by channel?
2. How do you reconcile DTC, TikTok Shop, Amazon and retail without double-counting demand?
3. What does the marketing leader spend time doing today that this role should absorb first?
4. Where does launch coordination most often break: decisions, inputs, handoffs, inventory or follow-through?
5. Who owns the current source of truth for the launch calendar?
6. How are retail launches integrated with DTC, CRM and social planning?
7. Which products acquire customers, and which ones create repeat behavior?
8. How do creative learnings move between paid, organic, TikTok Shop, affiliates, CRM and retail?
9. Does the team use a shared creative taxonomy today?
10. How are CAC, MER, LTV and repeat purchase defined and governed?
11. What percentage of recurring reporting is automated—and what still requires manual reconciliation?
12. What is the intended role of hair perfume in the portfolio: acquisition, frequency, gifting or category expansion?
13. How do you decide when a social signal is strong enough to change inventory or channel support?
14. Which retailer or marketplace feedback reaches product and creative teams fastest today?
15. What would make you say after 90 days that marketing is running on rhythm rather than heroics?
