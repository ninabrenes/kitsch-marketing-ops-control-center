# Operating Cadence

## Weekly

KPI pulse; launch readiness; blocker review; decision queue. Inputs lock before the meeting. Outputs are decisions, owners and deadlines—not a recap of activity.

## Monthly

Business review; budget pacing; channel performance; voice of customer; launch retrospectives; competitive and search intelligence. The meeting ends with stop, scale and standardize choices.

## Quarterly

OKRs; budget and resource allocation; launch roadmap; experiments; strategic priorities. Each priority has an owner, measurable outcome and explicit trade-off.

## Meeting rule

Every recurring meeting has a purpose, required inputs, decision rights, owner, timebox, output and follow-through mechanism.
